# Strategic Analysis Dimensions — Facebook Ads Library v3.8

## 1. Funnel
AI-classify the MESSAGE as Awareness / Consideration / Conversion / Mixed / Unknown.
Do not claim this is the actual Meta campaign objective unless sourced.

## 2. Content Mix
Classify creative clusters into:
- Promotional
- Product / Service
- Educational
- Social Proof
- Comparison
- Story / Narrative
- Objection Handling
- Demonstration
- Other

Report count and share.

## 3. Likely Audience Message
Infer who the copy appears designed to persuade.
Always label this as AI inference.
Never claim private targeting settings from copy alone.

## 4. Buying Strategy / Commercial Intent
AI may infer likely intent:
- Lead generation
- Traffic
- Direct response / conversion
- Sales
- Conversation / messaging
- Awareness / demand generation

Then recommend the KPI needed to validate it:
- Traffic => CPC / LPV / CVR
- Lead Gen => CPL / CPA / lead quality
- Sales => CPA / ROAS / AOV / CVR
- Messaging => cost per conversation / qualified conversation rate
- Awareness => reach / CPM / frequency / lift where available

Never invent the numeric KPI value from Ads Library.

## 5. Timing Strategy
Classify Seasonal / Evergreen / Event-based / Promotional deadline / Unknown.
Use copy and delivery dates as evidence.

## 6. Creative Format & Rotation
When visible, classify static / video / carousel / other.
Analyze rotation as:
- exact/near duplicate
- copy variant
- hook variant
- offer variant
- format variant
- materially new angle

## Creative Architecture
Deconstruct:
Hook → Problem → Desire → Angle → Mechanism → Value Proposition → Offer → Proof → CTA

## Strategic Pattern Questions
- What does the advertiser repeat most?
- Which stage of the funnel dominates?
- Which content type is over/underrepresented?
- Which audience message is repeated?
- What commercial action appears to be requested?
- Is the strategy seasonal or evergreen?
- How aggressively are creatives rotated?

## Creative Gap
Identify underused territories, then turn them into testable ideas.
A gap is not proof of superior performance.


# Creative Selection Strategy Framework

## Pain vs Gain
Classify each creative cluster as Pain-led / Gain-led / Mixed / Neutral.
Report both the portfolio share and the repeated specific pains/gains.

## Commercial Focus Model
Separate **Hero Product / Service**, **Hero Offer**, and **Hero Acquisition Mechanism**. Infer current advertising emphasis using both Ad Share and Unique Cluster Share, plus variant count, launch recency, repeated promises, offer structures, and cross-category reuse.

Do not force one Hero Product when the portfolio is Offer-led, Acquisition-mechanism-led, or Multi-category. Never call any item a best seller, winner, highest-ROAS, or most profitable without first-party proof.

## Creative Angle Portfolio
Tag major angles such as Pain, Gain, Urgency, Price, Authority, Social Proof, Demonstration, Education, Comparison, Objection Handling, Novel Mechanism, Convenience, Risk Reduction, FOMO, and Identity/Status.
For each angle explain its psychological job and likely funnel role.

## Creative Selection Logic
Assess:
- Exploit vs Explore
- Angle concentration vs diversification
- Hook variation vs angle variation
- Offer consistency vs offer testing
- Proof-heavy vs claim-heavy
- Funnel balance
- Seasonal vs evergreen mix
- Rotation cadence

## Strengths & Weaknesses
Every judgement must include:
- evidence
- interpretation
- confidence
- validation data needed

Do not equate repetition with success. Do not infer actual CPA/ROAS from creative choices.

## Creative Opportunity Map
Return four buckets:
- Keep / Adapt
- Test
- Avoid / Watch
- Validate

## Evidence Confidence
Before portfolio-level conclusions, check dataset coverage and ad-level observability. When coverage is limited, describe distributions as sample-level and phrase absences as `not observed in the retrieved sample`.
